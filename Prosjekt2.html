D-Day (Battle of Normandy) (1h 31min 44sek into the movie) TASK NR.2 The Battle of Normandy (D-Day) The year is 1944 and France is occupied by Nazi Germany. The Allied Forces (Britain, USA, Sovjet, China and the occupied France) are scrambling their armies for a huge battle in France, The Battle of Normandy. Their goal was to free Europe from Hitler reign of terror, but it wasn’t going to be easy. For 2 years The Allies gathered millions of soldiers and tons of food for what the Allies believed would be their greatest shot at freeing France and the rest of Europe. June 6 1944 arrived and over 156 thousand troops from the allied civilizations gathered at the coast of Britain and they sailed off for a chance of a free world. Boats and planes flew into French oceans and onto Normandy beach and a bloody battle followed. The Nazis were safely hiding in their fortified bunkers on the hills of Normandy and shot down the incoming allies fast. After hours of battling the allies were overwhelming for the Nazis and after a whole day of non-stop explosions and gunfire the beach was liberated from the Nazis. TASK NR.3 Alan Turing talks about how he and his team helped the allies win the war. He mentions the battle of Stalingrad, Battle of Normandy and the Ardennes. There’s some sort of calm, heroic music playing in the background while it’s showing real clips from the war and the battles mentioned. The music in this scene gives you a weird feeling of pain and calmness (that’s the best i can describe the feeling i get). The music sets the serious tone for this scene and I think it does it really well! What I really love is the fact that they used real black and white footage from the war, which I think was really a good decision. If they were to not use real footage and instead make footage with CGI and a lot of editing, I don't think the scene would have the same impact it did on me.
